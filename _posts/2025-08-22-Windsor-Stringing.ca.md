This project came about from my dad, who had just recently started a tennis racquet stringing business and wanted a website to display his services.

I had no prior experience with HTML or CSS, but I was curious how it worked so I told him I could do it. It was pretty easy to pick up, at least for what I wanted to do, and I very quickly asked him for his preferences so I knew what design to go for.

In the end, he only needed a nice front-end with a few different pages for products, services, contact information, and a gallery to show his past work.
