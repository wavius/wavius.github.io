As a small present for my friends and family over the holidays, I wanted to make something cute that was uniquely from me. In that sense, what could possibly be better than a PCB??

It of course needed to be something small and fun, and after a bit of thinking, I came up with an LED heart keychain. (🩵🩶)

At this point, I already had some PCB design experience, but I had never actually had one of my boards manufactured. I'd also only ever soldered wires before, and thought that this would be the perfect opportunity to practice with SMD components for other projects I was also working on at the time.

The SMD resistors were a little challenging to solder at first, but the LEDs were a nice break and I had fun learning. I'm really happy with how everything turned out and I think everyone else was too!! 

Even now I'm still quite fond of the purple solder mask 😼.

If you're looking for a cute gift, the files for this project are available on my [GitHub](https://github.com/wavius/PCB-Designs/tree/main).
